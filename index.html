How We Work

Strategy. Build.
Long-term Growth.

Every engagement follows a proven three-phase model — from custom roadmap to running store to continuous optimization.

01

Discuss

We start with your goals, your stack, and your market. Then we build a custom strategy and roadmap — no templates, no guesswork.

  • eCommerce strategy & roadmap
  • Platform selection (Magento vs Shopify)
  • CRO audit & opportunity mapping
  • Technical discovery
Start the conversation

02

Build

Responsive, optimized storefronts built with user-first interfaces. We handle Magento, Shopify, Hyvä, and everything in between.

  • Magento 2 & Hyvä theme development
  • Shopify & Shopify Plus builds
  • B2B & B2C commerce
  • Payment gateway & third-party integrations
  • AR/VR & Web2Print solutions
See platform expertise

Run

Your store goes live and then the real work begins. We provide ongoing support, speed optimization, and continuous feature delivery.

  • Conversion rate optimization
